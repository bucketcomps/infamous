Saber's real-PS3 graphics tests are in, and they're already paying off

Saber ran seven graphics tests on a real PS3, and every one came back complete. On every test the real console did something the emulator doesn't, in small but important ways: how it rounds colours, how its shader registers work, how it blends, and what a draw sees when it reads its own image.

  • Saber ran seven graphics tests on a real PS3, and every one came back complete. On every test the real console did something the emulator doesn't, in small but important ways: how it rounds colours, how its shader registers work, how it blends, and what a draw sees when it reads its own image.
  • This is why we test on real hardware. Those answers replace guesses in our renderer and our builds, so they draw the game the way a PS3 does, not the way an emulator does. An independent review re-checked every result before we act on it.
  • More tests are ready: three more are built and passed our checks, and a batch of seventeen small follow-up tests is being checked now. Thanks, Saber!
  • The verified count stays at 4,074 of 21,054 functions.

The crater in 4K, the start of our own graphics effects, and real-PS3 timing from Saber

The crater in 4K: our renderer drew the fully lit crater at 3840x2160 with the 4x texture pack. To get there we fixed how it scales up: the extra resolution used to trip one of its safety checks.

  • The crater in 4K: our renderer drew the fully lit crater at 3840x2160 with the 4x texture pack. To get there we fixed how it scales up: the extra resolution used to trip one of its safety checks.
  • Our own effects system: the renderer can now run extra graphics effects built right into it (no outside tools), switched on and tuned from a settings file and easy to add to. First up: tone mapping, bloom, ambient shadowing, fog and depth of field. The goal is everything our earlier Blender showpiece had, and more.
  • Honest result: the first effects looked worse — blurry and washed out. So for now anything that can blur is switched off: the default keeps only a colour/tone touch, and it measures at least as sharp as the plain image. Depth of field will come back only when it is done properly.
  • 4x textures were softer at normal resolution than the game's own textures, because their smaller versions were remade badly. Until the proper fix lands, normal-resolution images use the original textures; the 4x pack still shines at 4K.
  • Real-PS3 timing from Saber, our tester: measurements from a real console showed exactly how the display refreshes and when frames flip. They exposed a timing bug in our port, which is being fixed now. Thanks, Saber.
  • Three more decomp source contributions merged. The verified count stays at 4,074 of 21,054 functions.

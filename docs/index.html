          Correction: the "intro movie freeze" is not a proven stall when measured in game time

          Our boot runs the game in deep slow motion, so we've been judging it by the wrong clock (instructions) instead of the game's own clock, frame flips.

          • Our boot runs the game in deep slow motion, so we've been judging it by the wrong clock (instructions) instead of the game's own clock, frame flips.
          • In the real game (RPCS3 reference) the whole intro movie, Sony card plus Sucker Punch card, lasts 373 flips (12.5 seconds). In our boot the picture holds still from flip 23 to flip 135: 112 flips, well under the length of the movie. The Sony card is meant to sit still for a few seconds.
          • So the earlier "freeze at about 630M instructions" claims are not demonstrated stalls. They may just be the logo on screen in slow motion. Our runs were capped before they could reach flip 373 to check. A longer run, with logging on every movie-file read, is now under way to settle it.
          • New rule for all boot reports: judge progress in flips and game seconds against the real game, and call something a stall only if it clearly outlasts the reference.
